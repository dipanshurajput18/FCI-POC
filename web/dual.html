The rig (3D model · drag to rotate)

Open the 3D model full screen ↗

  • Rice spread on a clear glass plate, one phone above and one below.
  • Matte black card with a lens hole behind each phone, so each phone sees rice on black.
  • Calibration markers printed twice and glued back to back at the glass corners.
  • The bottom phone sees a mirror image; the app flips it back.
